# GQ Distributor (`HSGQDistributor`, `hs-modules/hs-qol-buttons/`)

Written from the code: earlier decisions about this feature were never written down; add them here when they come up.

`hs-gq-distributor.ts` (`HSGQDistributor`, created by `HSQOLButtons`) owns the panel and purchases; `hs-gq-distributor-plan.ts` owns the pure allocation; `hs-gq-purchase-budget.ts` encodes purchase budgets. `HSQOLButtons` shows the panel at each visit of Singularity → Shop when `enableGQDistributor` is on (setting action `enableGQDistributorAction`) and adds the "Buy MAX" button to the game's purchase dialog.

- **A "Distributor" button above the top left corner of the upgrade box (`#actualSingularityUpgradeContainer`) opens the panel**, and a minimize button closes it (disabled during a run, so the status isn't lost). Open or closed is kept (`gqDistributorOpen`, closed by default). No game element is moved: the button is absolutely placed, as a child of the box so it follows the box's width. Hovering it therefore triggers the game's hover look on the upgrades. Closed, a shop visit reads no save and doesn't scan.
- **Where:** under the game's `#goldenQuarksDisplay`, which stays visible, rebuilt at each shop visit while open (`#gqDistributorRenderVersion` drops a render overtaken by a newer one or by `hide()`). Its CSS is one injected block (`hs-gq-distributor-css`), flushed before drawing; only state styles stay inline (Distribute faded during a run, `display: none` on the open button while the panel is open).
- **Which upgrades:** the unmaxed GQ upgrades the player can see, found by clicking the game's hide-maxed toggle (`toggleMaxedGoldenQuarkUpgrades`) and reading `upgradeHiddenByMaxLevel`, then putting the toggle back; `isScanning()` tells `HSQOLButtons` those clicks aren't the player's (`hideMaxedGQUpgrades`, "Keep maxed GQ upgrades hidden": the game forgets its toggle at each load, so this setting and the Octeract one stay). Upgrades below their minimum singularity (`goldenQuarkUpgradeMinimumSingularity`) are left out.
- **Ratios:** one number per upgrade, saved exact in `gqDistributorRatios` (JSON, hidden setting) at each key; the inputs show 2 decimals. Missing = 0. "Match invested ratios" sets them to the GQ already invested, the largest at 100. "Reset" sets them all to 0.
- **Two modes** (`gqDistributorBalanceInvestments`, hidden setting, checkbox "Balance total investments"):
  - Off: only the GQ balance is split, in proportion to the ratios.
  - On: water-filling. The final invested totals track the ratios; upgrades already above their target get nothing and nothing is taken from them.
  - Both: amounts rounded by largest remainder, so the whole balance (rounded down) is planned.
- **Skipped before the allocation:** maxed since the list was built, or next level costing more than the whole balance (the game would answer those with an Alert instead of the purchase dialog). "Keep the share of skipped upgrades" (`gqDistributorKeepSkippedShare`): on, they still take part in the split and their share stays unspent; off, their share goes to the others.
- **Skipped during the purchases:** a share of 0 ("Already at or above its ratio" when balancing, else "Share too small"), or a share below the next level's cost (checked before the click: the game refuses with an Alert once the balance left can't buy a level), or the purchase dialog's OK disabled after typing the amount (cancelled). A share too small is never spent, whatever the checkbox. The status lists the skips by reason with the game's upgrade names, and stays until the next status so it can be copied; "Done!" clears after 3 s.
- **Purchases go through the game's dialog**, one shift-click per upgrade, inside one `HSGameDialogs.run('gqDistributor')` session that holds the dialog lock from the save snapshot (`getForcedGameData`, one per distribution) to the last purchase. Each click: `act({ purchasePrompt: 'show', alert: 'dismiss' })`, the amount typed into `#purchasePromptCost` (the game works out the levels and caps), OK under `act({ alert: 'dismiss' })` (multi-level and One Mind purchases chain an Alert). It stops with a status when the session's time limit is reached, another dialog opens, or the purchase dialog doesn't open within 2 s. Ratios are read once the lock is taken (the inputs stay editable while it waits for an open game dialog); the checkboxes and buttons are disabled for the run.
- **Log line:** each distribution logs `GQ Distributor: budget=… plannedTotal=… unallocated=… planned=[{id, weight, invested, spend}]` as a normal line.
- **Purchase budgets** use ASCII digits with an integer significand (no decimal or grouping separator), so the game's locale-aware parser reads even scientific-notation amounts exactly. Before OK, dispatch `change` to obtain the game's unrounded raw quote from the cost field; cancel and stop with a logged error if it is nonfinite, nonpositive or above the allocation. Never verify spending against the rounded summary text.
- **Checking a change to the allocation:** the old and new code side by side in node on random inputs (both modes, Keep share on/off, unbuyable upgrades, balances up to 1e13); done for the plan file's extraction, 200k cases, no difference.
